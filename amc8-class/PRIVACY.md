# Math Hoops privacy

**Who has an account:** only parents or guardians. They sign in with their email address.
Kids never create accounts and never enter an email.

**What we store:**
- the parent's email address, for sign-in and the daily report
- each player's **nickname** and jersey number, chosen by the parent (no full names needed)
- game progress: scores, topics practiced, streaks, rewards, and the mistake types the
  player tags

**What we don't store:** birthdays, photos, locations, school names, or anything typed
outside the game.

**Who can see it:** only the signed-in parent, for their own family. The database enforces
this. Parent emails are used only for sign-in links and the daily report the parent turns on.

**No ads, no tracking, no selling:** none of this data is shared with anyone.

**Deleting data:** on the Family screen, **Delete my family's data** permanently removes
the account, all players and all progress. Players can also use the app with no account
("Play on this device only"); then nothing leaves the device.

**Services used:**
- **Supabase**, to store the data
- **Resend**, to deliver emails
- **GitHub Pages**, to host the app

Questions: contact the site owner through the GitHub repository.
